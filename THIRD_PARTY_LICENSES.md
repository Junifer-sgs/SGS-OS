## Review checklist

For every third-party item, record:

- Exact name and version, if applicable.
- Creator, provider, or official source.
- Where it is used in SGS OS.
- The applicable license or terms-of-service URL.
- Whether attribution, notices, source disclosure, or other conditions are required.
- Whether commercial use, modification, and redistribution are permitted.

Do not list an API as SGS-owned merely because SGS OS connects to it. API access is governed by the provider's terms and may require keys, attribution, or other conditions.

If the license or permission status of an item is unknown, mark it **Unverified** and investigate before distributing it.
